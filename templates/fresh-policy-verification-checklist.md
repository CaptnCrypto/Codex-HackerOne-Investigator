# Fresh program-policy verification checklist

Complete manually immediately before enabling any network-capable workflow. Keep the
completed checklist and policy snapshot private.

- [ ] Confirm the program is open and the researcher remains eligible.
- [ ] Save the current policy and structured scope privately; record timestamp and SHA-256.
- [ ] Confirm safe-harbor, confidentiality, and disclosure terms.
- [ ] Confirm whether automation and AI-assisted research are allowed.
- [ ] Record the strictest current request-rate and concurrency limits.
- [ ] Confirm the exact owned test hostname or asset is explicitly in scope.
- [ ] Record excluded assets, features, techniques, and third-party infrastructure.
- [ ] Confirm rules for customer data, credentials, code execution, and service degradation.
- [ ] Confirm evidence and reporting requirements.
- [ ] Compare the current policy with the previous private snapshot and record changes.
- [ ] Set a short expiration time for the verification record.
- [ ] Obtain explicit human approval for every exact hostname, URL, method, and hypothesis.
